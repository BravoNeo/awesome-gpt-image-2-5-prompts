The MIT license applies to repository code, schema and automation written for this project. It does not grant a license to third-party prompts, images, author names or source posts.

Each artwork credits its original author and links to the original public post. Third-party images are referenced by their remote public URLs and are not downloaded or rehosted by this repository. Their authors retain ownership; public availability is not a blanket permission for redistribution, commercial use, or relicensing.

The `rights` field records the contribution basis. `third-party-source` means the work is attributed through its public source; it grants no new license. `submitter-permission` records a contributor's permission to submit the prompt and media links, while preserving original ownership. It does not automatically apply MIT or CC BY to the work.

For attribution corrections or removal, open an issue with the original source URL and the requested change, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
